## Decisions

- Existing Weebly forms were replaced by honest email-draft forms. Their backend was not copied or reverse-engineered.
- Video previews use the choir's existing photographs. Video titles are neutral labels because the old page did not provide individual titles.
- Photos with photographer names in the source filenames retain those credits. All original URLs are preserved in `media-sources.json`.
- Four large source background images were also imported for page headers. Original photographs are preserved without AI alteration.
- The old homepage sponsor-logo link and expired Twitter link were not promoted into the new design. The new site provides the active Instagram and Facebook destinations.
- The source privacy policy was offered on request rather than published. The new privacy page describes the actual website behaviour and explains how to request the choir's policy.
- Donation account details and other operational facts should be maintained by the choir when they change.
